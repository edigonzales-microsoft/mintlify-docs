VRS lets you reduce pixel shader work in regions of the frame where full-rate shading isn't needed.

## VRS tiers on XBOX

- **Tier 1** — per-draw shading rate.
- **Tier 2** — per-primitive and screen-space shading-rate images.

## Common uses

- **Foveated rendering** — full rate in the center, coarser at the edges.
- **Motion-based VRS** — coarser rate where motion vectors are large.
- **Content-adaptive VRS** — coarser rate in low-contrast areas.
