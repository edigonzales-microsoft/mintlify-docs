Mesh shaders replace the legacy vertex / geometry / tessellation pipeline with two new stages: **amplification** and **mesh**.

## Why use them

- Cull at meshlet granularity, before the rasterizer.
- Procedurally generate geometry on the GPU without round-trip CPU buffers.
- Bypass input-assembler limits for huge instanced scenes.

## Best practices on XBOX Series X|S

- Pack vertices into ~64-vertex / ~126-primitive meshlets.
- Use the amplification shader for instance culling, LOD selection, and meshlet visibility tests.
- Combine with [VRS](/gdk/vrs) and [sampler feedback](/gdk/sampler-feedback) for further savings.
